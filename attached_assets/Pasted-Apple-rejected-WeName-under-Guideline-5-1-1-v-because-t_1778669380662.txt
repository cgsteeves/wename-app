Apple rejected WeName under Guideline 5.1.1(v) because the app requires users to register/sign in before purchasing in-app purchase products that Apple considers not account-based.

We need to revise the premium purchase flow so guest users can purchase Premium through Apple IAP without being forced to create an account first.

Important:
Do not remove account creation entirely. Account creation should remain optional and useful for syncing, partner linking, backup, and restoring across devices. But it must not be required before buying Premium.

Required behavior:

1. Guest users can buy Premium
- If a guest taps Upgrade/Premium, do not force sign-in first.
- Show the RevenueCat/Apple purchase flow directly.
- Call Purchases.purchasePackage(package) for the current anonymous RevenueCat user.
- If Apple payment succeeds and RevenueCat returns active entitlement "premium", unlock Premium for that guest/device.
- Do not require email, Google, or Apple sign-in before purchase.

2. Keep account creation optional after purchase
After successful guest purchase, show a prompt like:
"Premium is active on this device. Create a free account to back up your names, sync with a partner, and restore Premium on other devices."

Buttons:
- "Create account"
- "Maybe later"

3. Explain benefits of account creation
Account creation may be recommended for:
- restoring purchase on other devices
- syncing liked names/matches
- partner linking
- backing up names

But it must be optional.

4. RevenueCat identity behavior
- For guests, let RevenueCat use the anonymous App User ID.
- If the guest later creates/signs into an account, call Purchases.logIn(supabaseUser.id).
- If RevenueCat transfers the anonymous purchase to the signed-in user, treat that as expected restore/transfer behavior.
- Do not lose entitlement during guest-to-account transition.

5. Restore Purchases
- Keep Restore Purchases available without forcing sign-in.
- If RevenueCat restorePurchases returns active entitlement "premium", unlock Premium.
- After restore, optionally prompt the user to create an account for cross-device access.

6. Premium gating
- Premium access remains based only on RevenueCat:
  customerInfo.entitlements.active["premium"]
- Do not unlock premium from Supabase plan_tier alone.
- Do not unlock premium simply because the user signed in.
- Do not unlock premium simply because the purchase button was tapped.

7. Update UI copy
Replace any mandatory language like:
"Sign in to purchase Premium"

With optional language like:
"Create an account to sync Premium and your names across devices."

For guests on the Premium screen:
Primary CTA:
"Continue with Apple Purchase"

Secondary copy:
"No account required to purchase. Create an account anytime to sync across devices."

8. Adjust previous purchase flow
Previous flow:
guest → auth required → purchase

New flow:
guest → purchase directly → optional account creation after purchase

Signed-in users:
signed-in → purchase directly

9. App Review compliance
Add app review notes explaining:
- Premium can now be purchased without creating an account.
- Account creation is optional and only used for syncing, partner linking, backup, and cross-device restore.

10. Test cases
Verify:
A) Guest can open Premium and purchase without sign-in.
B) Guest purchase unlocks Premium after RevenueCat entitlement is active.
C) Guest can dismiss account creation prompt and still use Premium on device.
D) Guest can later create account and entitlement transfers/restores correctly.
E) Signed-in users can still purchase Premium normally.
F) Restore Purchases works without requiring sign-in.
G) Premium remains locked if RevenueCat entitlement is inactive.
H) Supabase plan_tier alone does not unlock Premium.

Do not modify unrelated magic link auth, AI suggestions, name packs, swipe mechanics, partner linking, or visual styling unless directly needed for this compliance fix.
