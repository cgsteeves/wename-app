Support

We're here to help.

Connecting with your partner

Open the Names tab, tap the partner icon, and share your invite link or code. Your partner enters the code in their app to link your accounts.

Seeing your matches

Matches appear in the "Shared Matches" tab after both you and your partner swipe right on the same name.

Forgot your password

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and enter your email address. You'll receive a reset link within a few minutes.

Restoring a purchase

Go to Settings → Restore Purchases. This will restore your WeName Premium access if you previously purchased on this Apple ID.

Deleting your account

Go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

Still need help?
