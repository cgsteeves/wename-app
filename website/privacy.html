Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2025

What we collect

WeName collects your email address (used for account creation), baby name preferences you swipe on, and names matched with your partner. We do not collect any sensitive personal information.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the WeName service — matching your name preferences with your partner's and generating AI suggestions. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Data storage

Your data is stored securely using Supabase (PostgreSQL) with industry-standard encryption. Data is hosted in the EU.

Third-party services

WeName uses the following third-party services:

Supabase — database and authentication. Privacy Policy

RevenueCat — in-app purchase management. Privacy Policy

Data deletion

You can delete your account and all associated data at any time from the app's Settings screen. To request data deletion, contact us at hello@wename.app.

Children

WeName is intended for adults who are expecting a baby or planning a family. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email us at hello@wename.app.
